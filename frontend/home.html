Everything you need to manage the agricultural supply chain

Built for DA field officers, LGU agricultural planners, AEWs, cooperatives, farmers, and buyers.

Price Forecasting

Monitor and forecast farmgate prices per commodity and region using PSA/DA-AMAD data.

Oversupply Alerts

Automatic alerts when supply exceeds 120% of projected demand with recommended actions.

Geospatial Map

Interactive municipality-level supply-demand map with color-coded surplus/deficit status.

Buyer Matching

Connect smallholder farmers with verified institutional buyers and trading cooperatives.

Planting Intent Registry

Collect and track farmer-declared crop production plans with harvest date timelines.

Supply-Demand Analytics

Deep barangay-level analysis of commodity supply ratios with exportable reports.

Step 01

Register

AEWs register farmers and record planting intents. Buyers complete verification to access the marketplace securely.

Step 02

Monitor

Real-time dashboards show price trends, supply-demand maps, and automated oversupply/deficit alerts by region.

Step 03

Act

Farmers and buyers connect through offtake requests. DA officers intervene proactively with evidence-based advisories.
